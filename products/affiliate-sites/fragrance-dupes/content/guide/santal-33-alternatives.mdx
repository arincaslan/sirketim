[Le Labo Santal 33](/fragrance/santal-33/) is a Woody eau de parfum that became a signature of its decade. Our catalogue puts **cardamom, violet and iris** on top, **sandalwood and papyrus** in the heart, and **leather, cedarwood and musk** in the base.

It rates **9/10 for woody depth**, **7/10 for longevity** and **6/10 for sillage**. Our catalogue lists its longevity at roughly six to eight hours and its sillage as strong. The appeal is a dry, creamy wood with a leathery edge and a powdery violet-iris lift, which is why people ask for it by name.

## What is actually in it

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Cardamom, Violet, Iris |
| Heart | Sandalwood, Papyrus |
| Base | Leather, Cedarwood, Musk |

Eight materials, and the expensive-sounding one, sandalwood, is the centre. Papyrus adds a dry, paper-like woodiness, and cedarwood and leather give it a smoky, tanned base. Whatever you buy in its place has to land that creamy-dry wood to count as an alternative, and the iris-and-violet opening is what stops it from reading as simply a woody scent.

## The one listed alternative

This is the first Santal 33 alternative we list, and it is the only one.

**Pleasure Noir No. 63 by The CLONE** is paired with Santal 33 by the seller, not by us. The retailer's listing describes it as a modern clone fragrance inspired by Santal 33, blending dry woods, soft florals and a clean leather finish, and that is the seller's claim.

The seller declares **violet, cardamom, iris, ambroxan, papyrus, cedarwood, leather and sandalwood**. Compared with our catalogue entry:

- **Almost every signature material is present**: cardamom, violet, iris, papyrus, sandalwood, cedarwood and leather.
- **Musk is not declared.** That is the main omission.
- **Ambroxan is added.** It is a modern, clean, slightly salty amber-woody material that our entry for Santal 33 does not list.

Our verdict is that this is a re-stacking of the same materials rather than a copy, which is a fair thing to want as long as you know it is what you are buying.

One caution about how this listing is described. The seller publishes a flat list of materials, not a pyramid. The split into top, heart and base on our listing is ours, and the listing's pyramid is imputed, not declared. For that reason we make no claims here about which layer the seller puts a note in, and we do not claim to know how it unfolds on skin.

## Why "same notes" does not settle it

- **Order and proportion are the recipe.** The same eight ingredients in different doses make a different scent. A list carries neither.
- **Ambroxan changes the texture.** It tends to make a woody fragrance feel cleaner and carry further. That can be welcome or can move it away from the original's creamier feel.
- **Concentration is a separate variable.** The original is an eau de parfum. The listing is described by its seller as an extrait de parfum. More concentrated does not mean closer, and what a seller calls a concentration is the seller's description rather than something we have tested.

## How to decide

**You want the sandalwood, iris and leather combination for less.** The listed alternative declares almost all of the original's signature materials. Read it as a close relative.

**You love the original's soft musky finish.** Musk is the one declared omission, so this is the part to be careful about.

**You want the mood and not the match.** The originals below take other routes to a woody, leathery, dry scent.

## Where claims come from

The notes and facets for Santal 33 are our catalogue's. The note list for Pleasure Noir No. 63 is the one its seller published, and the pairing is the seller's own description. We have not smelled either bottle, and the listing has not been independently verified, which is why our published scores are capped. [How we score is on our standards page](/about/#methodology).

## What to check before you buy

- **Read the full note list.** Look for what the listing adds, here ambroxan, and what it omits, here musk.
- **Check the concentration and bottle size** on the product page, and compare like with like before judging value.
- **Be sceptical of marketing claims** about strength or longevity. These are the seller's words, not our findings.
- **Patch-test** a new fragrance on a small area of skin before you commit to a full bottle.
