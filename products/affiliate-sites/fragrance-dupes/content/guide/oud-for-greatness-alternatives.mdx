[Initio Oud for Greatness](/fragrance/oud-for-greatness/) is an Oriental Woody. The catalogue pyramid opens on **saffron, nutmeg and lavender**, puts **oud and patchouli** in the heart, and rests on **musk and sandalwood**.

It is built for strength. In our catalogue it rates **9/10 for longevity**, **9/10 for sillage** and **9/10 for woody depth**, with very little freshness or sweetness getting in the way. That profile is the thing to match when you look at alternatives: a lighter oud is a different purchase.

## What is actually in it

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Saffron, Nutmeg, Lavender |
| Heart | Oud, Patchouli |
| Base | Musk, Sandalwood |

The opening is unusual for an oud. Lavender and saffron are bright and aromatic, nutmeg adds spice, and together they give the dark heart something to push against. The sandalwood in the base is what rounds the dry-down. Keep that last point in mind, because both alternatives below leave it out.

## Two listings, both paired by the seller

We list two alternatives, and in both cases the pairing is the seller's claim, not our editorial judgement.

**Bade'e Al Oud Oud For Glory by Lattafa** is described by the retailer as "inspired by initio oud for greatness". Its published pyramid has lavender, saffron and nutmeg on top, oud and patchouli in the heart, and oud, patchouli and musk in the base. Our verdict is that it is the closest thing to a straight substitution in the category: the opening and the smoky oud-patchouli heart track the original closely, and it projects hard. What it gives up is the sandalwood, which is where the original turns creamy. This one stays dry and smoky throughout.

**Oud Is Great by Zimaya** is the other. This one needs a careful reading, because the retailer's listing has no "inspired by" tagline. It is paired on a longer product description that says it "captures the essence of luxury and refinement of the renowned Initio Oud for Greatness, with its own twist, focusing on patchouli and nutmeg". That is the seller's wording and the seller's claim. Its pyramid carries saffron, nutmeg and lavender on top, agarwood and oud in the heart, and patchouli and musk in the base. It is sold as an extrait de parfum, where the original is an eau de parfum. Our verdict is that the saffron, nutmeg and lavender opening carries over exactly, while the sandalwood drops out and the patchouli moves into the base, so the dry-down is more linear than the original's.

## What the two have in common

Both keep the opening and the oud. Both drop the sandalwood. If the creamy, woody finish is what you like about Oud for Greatness, neither listing is built to deliver it. If the saffron-lavender-oud arc is what you like, both are on the right track.

## Where they differ

The difference is mostly in where patchouli sits and how it is labelled. Oud For Glory keeps patchouli in the heart beside the oud, as the original does, and repeats it in the base. Oud Is Great moves patchouli into the base and puts agarwood beside the oud in the heart. In practice, expect the Lattafa to track the original's middle more closely and the Zimaya to feel more like a straight line from oud to patchouli to musk. That is a reading of the published notes, not a wear test.

## How to decide

**You want the closest structure.** Oud For Glory follows the original's heart most directly.

**You want a stronger concentration.** Oud Is Great is listed as an extrait, which is a higher concentration than an eau de parfum, and that may change how it wears.

**You care about the sandalwood.** Neither listing has it. Look at a different route, such as the [Oud Wood alternatives](/guide/oud-wood-alternatives/), where creamy woods are the point.

## Where claims come from

The notes and facets for Oud for Greatness are our catalogue's. The pyramids for both alternatives are the ones their sellers published, and both pairings are the sellers' own descriptions. We have not smelled any of the three, and neither alternative's pyramid has been independently verified, which is why our published scores are capped. [How we score is on our standards page](/about/#methodology).

## What to check before you buy

- **Look for the sandalwood.** If a listing leaves it out, the finish will be drier.
- **Check the concentration.** Extrait and eau de parfum are different formulations.
- **Read where patchouli sits.** Heart or base changes the shape of the middle.
- **Patch-test** a new fragrance on a small area of skin first.

## Neighbouring originals worth knowing

- **[Oud Wood](/fragrance/oud-wood/)** by Tom Ford is a quieter, creamier oud with rosewood and cardamom on top.
- **[Royal Oud](/fragrance/royal-oud/)** by Creed pairs oud with cedar and angelica under a pink pepper and bergamot top.
- **[Arabians Tonka](/fragrance/arabians-tonka/)** by Montale pairs saffron and rose with oud and tonka bean.
