[Mancera Red Tobacco](/fragrance/red-tobacco/) is an Oriental Spicy eau de parfum that sets out to be big. Our catalogue lists its longevity at roughly nine to twelve hours and its sillage as Beast Mode, which is the top of our scale.

It puts **cinnamon, saffron and nutmeg** on top, **tobacco, oud and leather** in the heart, and **vanilla, amber and musk** in the base. It rates **2/10 for freshness**, **9/10 for warmth** and **8/10 for woodiness**.

## What is actually in it

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Cinnamon, Saffron, Nutmeg |
| Heart | Tobacco, Oud, Leather |
| Base | Vanilla, Amber, Musk |

Nine materials, which is short for a fragrance this loud. The spices make the opening hot, the tobacco-oud-leather heart gives it a dark, smoky, hide-like body, and vanilla and amber sweeten the end without softening it.

Leather matters here. It is easy to read the scent as a tobacco-and-spice fragrance, but the leather is part of what gives it a dry, animalic edge.

## The one listed alternative

We list one alternative for this fragrance.

**Robust by AromaPassions** is paired with Red Tobacco by the seller. Its product title reads "Inspired by MANCERA RED TOBACCO", and that is the seller's claim.

The seller publishes a single flat list rather than a pyramid. It includes **cinnamon, oud, incense, saffron, nutmeg, green apple, white pear, patchouli, jasmine, tobacco, vanilla, amber, guaiac wood, sandalwood, white musk and vetiver**.

- **The core is declared.** Cinnamon, saffron, nutmeg, tobacco, oud, vanilla and amber all appear, with a white musk where the original has a plain musk.
- **Leather is not on the list.** That is the most important omission.
- **Several materials have been added**, including incense, patchouli, guaiac wood, sandalwood and vetiver, which push the scent toward woods and smoke.
- **Fruit and a white flower appear** (green apple, white pear, jasmine), which the original does not carry.

Our verdict is that the score understates the relationship, because a longer list dilutes each comparison. The expectation is the same spiced-tobacco direction, woodier and less hide-like. The seller describes it as an extrait de parfum; that is their description, not something we have tested.

Because AromaPassions publishes only a flat list, the split into top, heart and base on our listing is ours. We make no claim here that depends on which layer a note sits in, and the score carries a 10-point penalty for that reason. We have not smelled either bottle.

## Reading a long list against a short one

The original names nine materials and the listing names sixteen. A longer list is not a richer scent by default. When a list grows, each material tends to get a smaller share, and the spices that define the opening can end up as accents rather than the lead. It is worth reading the order the seller gives, while remembering that a flat list is not a pyramid and the order is not a promise.

## Why a dense scent is hard to copy

- **Proportions decide everything.** A list cannot say whether cinnamon or vanilla dominates.
- **Oud and leather are impressions.** Houses build them from different materials, sometimes synthetic, so two "oud" bottles can differ greatly.
- **More notes is not closer.** Adding woods and fruit can move a copy further from a short, dense original.

## How to decide

**You want the spiced tobacco and oud character.** The listing declares both, so it is a reasonable place to start.

**You want the leathery edge.** Be cautious. It is not declared.

**You want a different route to the same mood.** The originals below cover nearby ground.

## Where claims come from

The notes and facets for Red Tobacco are our catalogue's. The note list for Robust is the one its seller published, and the pairing is the seller's own description. The listing has not been independently verified, which is why our published scores are capped. [How we score is on our standards page](/about/#methodology).

## What to check before you buy

- **Read the full note list.** Look for leather, and for added fruit and woods.
- **Check the concentration and bottle size** on the product page.
- **Be sceptical of marketing claims** about strength or longevity. These are the seller's words, not our findings.
- **Patch-test** a new fragrance on a small area of skin before you commit to a full bottle.
