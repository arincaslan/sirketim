[Tom Ford Tuscan Leather](/fragrance/tuscan-leather/) is a Leather fragrance with an unusual top. The catalogue pyramid opens on **raspberry, saffron and thyme**, puts **leather, jasmine and olibanum** in the heart, and rests on **suede, amber and woody notes**.

In our catalogue it rates **8/10 for longevity**, **8/10 for sillage** and **8/10 for warmth**. It is a heavy fragrance, and the fruit on top is what makes people react to it so strongly: some love it, some cannot wear it.

## What is actually in it

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Raspberry, Saffron, Thyme |
| Heart | Leather, Jasmine, Olibanum |
| Base | Suede, Amber, Woody Notes |

Leather fragrances usually try to smell dry and smoky. This one starts with fruit and herb, which is why a plain "leather" dupe is rarely a plain match. Whether an alternative has raspberry is the first question to ask.

## The one listed alternative

We list one alternative, and unlike some pairings on this site, this one is our own editorial judgement. No retailer describes it as a Tuscan Leather alternative.

**Al Areeq Gold by Lattafa** is a leather fragrance with **saffron and black tea** on top, **incense and suede** in the heart, and **amberwood, vanilla and musk** in the base. That pyramid is the one its seller published. Our verdict is that saffron over suede with incense underneath is the spine of Tuscan Leather, and this has all three of those materials.

The missing piece is the raspberry. It gives the Tom Ford its odd, fruity quality, and black tea stands in its place here. That makes this one drier and more conventional. It also means it will probably be easier to wear in places where the original's fruit would be too much. Note too that the layers differ: Tuscan Leather in our catalogue has suede in the base, while the seller lists suede in the heart, so do not expect the same order of events.

## Why the pairing is ours, and what that means

When a seller says "inspired by", the pairing is theirs and we attribute it to them. Here nobody has said it, so the comparison is our reading of the notes. That is a weaker kind of evidence, and the verdict on the listing reflects it. If you want a guarantee that a seller intended a match, this is not it.

## Reading the two pyramids side by side

Lay the lists next to each other and the shared ground is saffron, suede and a smoky resin: olibanum in the original, incense in the alternative. Both end warm and woody, and both rate high for warmth in our catalogue. The original then adds raspberry and thyme on top, and jasmine in the heart. The alternative adds black tea on top, and vanilla and musk in the base. Those additions are the reason the two will not smell the same, and they are worth reading before you decide whether the overlap is enough for you.

Because both are described by notes alone, it is worth remembering how little a note list tells you about proportion. Two fragrances can share a word and use it in very different amounts, so treat the overlap as a reason to look closer and not as a promise.

## How to decide

**You want the leather and saffron without the fruit.** Al Areeq Gold keeps that part and is the dry reading of the idea.

**The raspberry is what you love.** Look elsewhere. Nothing we list replaces it, and a listing that does not name raspberry will not smell like the original's opening.

**You find Tuscan Leather too much.** The listed alternative may suit you better than the original. That is a reason to consider it, not a flaw.

## Where claims come from

The notes and facets for Tuscan Leather are our catalogue's. The pyramid for Al Areeq Gold is the one its seller published. We have not smelled either bottle, and the pyramid has not been independently verified, which is why our published scores are capped. [How we score is on our standards page](/about/#methodology).

## What to check before you buy

- **Look for a fruit note on top.** Without it the opening will not match.
- **Check the leather source.** Suede and leather are named separately in these pyramids.
- **Check the base.** Amberwood and vanilla read warmer and sweeter than a plain woody base.
- **Patch-test** a new fragrance on a small area of skin first.

## Neighbouring originals worth knowing

- **[White Suede](/fragrance/white-suede/)** by Tom Ford keeps saffron and thyme on top and puts suede, rose and cumin in the heart.
- **[Ombre Leather](/fragrance/ombre-leather/)** by Tom Ford takes leather in a floral direction, with jasmine sambac and patchouli.
- **[Saffron Leather](/fragrance/saffron-leather/)** by IRFE puts saffron in the heart and leather in the base.
