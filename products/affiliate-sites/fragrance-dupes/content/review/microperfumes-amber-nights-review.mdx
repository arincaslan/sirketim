MicroPerfumes builds its whole catalog around travel-size formats, and Amber
Nights is its answer to the amber-woody category Baccarat Rouge 540 made
famous. It's the cheapest entry point in our dataset for this family, which
raises an obvious question: what do you actually give up at this price.

## Two weeks of wear

We wore Amber Nights on alternating days against Ambrosia (Dossier's take on
the same reference) for two weeks. The saffron opening is present but
quieter, more of a suggestion than the sharp, resinous hit the original
leads with. Where it holds its own is the middle stretch: the amber-floral
heart is recognizably in the right family, even if it's simpler than either
the original or Dossier's version.

By hour five, projection has mostly dropped to skin-scent range. For an
office day where you don't want to be smelled from across the room, that's
arguably a feature. For an evening-out scent meant to fill a space, it isn't
enough.
