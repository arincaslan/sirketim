Black Opium built an entire sub-genre of "coffee gourmand" fragrances, and
Bright is one of the more talked-about attempts at it in the sub-$40
bracket. The question worth answering isn't "does it smell similar," it's
which part of Black Opium actually survives the translation.

<EmbeddedComparison referenceSlug="black-opium" dupeSlug="alt-bright" />

## Where it earns its price

The coffee-vanilla heart, the part most people associate with Black Opium in
the first place, is the strongest part of this match. Wear them side by
side for a few hours and the middle stretch is genuinely close.

The opening is where they part ways. Black Opium leans on bitter almond and
pink pepper to keep the sweetness from tipping into simple, and Bright skips
most of that, so it reads sweeter and a little less complicated from the
first spray.
