## What the numbers don't tell you

The overlap score above is real, but a percentage can't tell you that
Ambrosia's opening is genuinely convincing. Spray both on opposite wrists and
for the first twenty minutes, most people won't be able to pick the original
out of a lineup. Where they diverge is the drydown: Baccarat Rouge 540's fir
resin base keeps developing for hours, while Ambrosia settles into a simpler,
sweeter amber and stays there.

That's not a flaw exactly, it's a different product at a different price
point. If you're buying to test whether the accord works on your skin before
committing to a full bottle of the original, Ambrosia is a genuinely useful
way to find out.
