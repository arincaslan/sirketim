### The host comparison (done 27 Aug, against each vendor's own pricing page)

**Chosen: Cloudflare Workers via static export** — not Pages. Cloudflare no longer creates Pages projects for new sites, and the distinction is load-bearing: a `functions/` directory does nothing on Workers, and `_redirects` rules are not applied to requests served by Worker code. A grep of `app/` and `lib/` found **no dynamic server surface at all** — no `cookies()`, `headers()`, `force-dynamic` or `revalidate`. The single route handler (`/go/[slug]`) was replaced by `scripts/generate-redirects.mjs`, which writes `public/_redirects` at build time; **the project now has zero route handlers.** Cloud Startup's 4 CPU / 4 GB would have been serving a folder of HTML.

## The integrity problem — the scoring half is now fully addressed; the data half never can be

> **Updated 8 Sep.** Everything below was written when the only defences were the verbatim-copy gate and the 90% unverified cap. Four more shipped, as one change, ahead of the producer-subscription program that would have made outside producers a second and larger source of the same self-reported data — decision record in `PRODUCER-PROGRAM.md` §7:
>
> - **A 95% structural ceiling on every published score**, `verified` included (previously uncapped). Identical declared notes never means identical proportions, and no formula over presence/absence data can certify otherwise.
> - **A flat −10 on any listing whose note-tier split is ours rather than the seller's** (47 of 79). Our own split can be arranged to flatter a score in a way a seller's published pyramid cannot.
> - **`familyBonus` fixed** — no longer hardcoded to `1`. A no-op for all 79 current listings by construction.
> - **A 15% ingredient/INCI component**, flat and untiered, inert until both sides publish a list. None do yet; the backfill (~144 lookups) is the open follow-on.
>
> ~~**A disclosed founder override is the only way past 95**~~ — **REMOVED 18 Sep, founder. Nothing goes past 95 now; the ceiling has no exceptions at all.** It was a human number with a mandatory written justification, its own badge, never on a house product, and unable to rescue a flagged copy. It was never used on any listing, so no published score changed when it went. Reasoning in `PRODUCER-PROGRAM.md` §7 item 5: every fence around it was an admission that the thing inside was dangerous, and an exception only the house can invoke is the part of a published rule a reader cannot check.
>
> **Measured:** the 32 declared listings moved by exactly zero, 41 of 47 imputed fell exactly 10, six fell less because they already sat at the 90 cap. One ranking changed site-wide — the Clone of Perfume listing this repo already called suspiciously #1 on Aventus is now #3, behind two declared-pyramid listings. **What is still not solved:** the penalty prices a *missing* pyramid, not copying. A merchant who copies the reference's pyramid and publishes it as three proper tiers is still scored at face value, and `isVerbatimCopy()` — which needs facets to match too — remains the only defence against that case.



`No. 01 Ember` — our own product — rendered **#1 at 79%** on Baccarat Rouge 540, ahead of every real listing. Verified against the live server, not inferred.

**✅ The scoring half is fixed (27 Aug).** A house product can no longer publish above the unverified cap however its status field reads, so it cannot display a number that only an independent check is allowed to earn; and the ranking can no longer invert against the displayed scores.
