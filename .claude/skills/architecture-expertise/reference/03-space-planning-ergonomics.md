# Space planning and ergonomics

The figures below are **standard professional dimensions and rules of thumb**, from Neufert-type data and common Turkish practice. Legal minimums are a separate, lower bar, and live only in `09-regulation-quick-reference.md`: room sizes (PAİY Madde 29), heights (Madde 28), stairs (Madde 31), doors (Madde 39). **Design to comfort; check against the minimum.**

## 1. The body and movement

| Situation | Width / clearance |
|---|---|
| One person walking | 0.60–0.75 m |
| Two people passing | 1.10–1.20 m |
| Person with a wheelchair | 0.80–0.90 m to pass; Ø 1.50 m turning circle |
| Standing in front of a counter, basin or wardrobe | 0.60–0.70 m (minimum); 0.90 m to pass behind someone |
| Behind a dining chair | 0.60 m to sit and stand up; 0.90 m to walk past a seated person |
| Eye height | standing about 1.50–1.65 m; seated about 1.10–1.20 m |
| Comfortable reach | shelves up to about 1.80 m; worktop 0.85–0.92 m high |

## 2. Furniture sizes

| Item | Typical size (cm) | Clearance to plan |
|---|---|---|
| Double bed | 160 × 200 (also 150, 180 wide) | 60–70 on both long sides; 90+ at the foot if a wardrobe faces it |
| Single bed | 90 × 200 (children 90 × 190) | 60 on the access side |
| Bedside table | 40–50 wide | — |
| Wardrobe | 58–60 deep, 210–240 high | 90 in front for hinged doors, about 70 for sliding |
| 3-seat sofa | 200–230 × 90–100 | 40–50 to the coffee table; 80–90 behind or beside for walking |
| Armchair | 80–90 × 80–90 | — |
| TV viewing distance | about 2.5–3.5 m for a 55–65" screen | — |
| Dining table, 4 people | 80 × 120 to 90 × 140 | 60 per person along the edge; 75–90 behind chairs |
| Dining table, 6 people | 90 × 180 to 100 × 200 | as above |
| Desk | 120–160 × 60–80, 75 high | 90–100 for the chair |
| Washing machine / dryer | 60 × 60, stackable | 60–70 in front to load |

## 3. Kitchen

- **Standard dimensions:**
  - base units 60 deep, worktop 90 high (85–92), plinth 10
  - wall units 30–35 deep, mounted 50–60 above the worktop
  - module widths 15, 30, 40, 45, 60, 80, 90
- **Appliances:** fridge 60–70 wide (90 for side-by-side), dishwasher 60 (45 slim), oven 60, hob 60/75/90, sink unit 80–90.
- **Clearance between facing runs:** 1.20 m (1.00 m minimum; 1.50 m for two cooks or a wheelchair). An island needs 1.00–1.20 m on every working side.
- **Work triangle (fridge, sink, hob):** each leg about 1.2–2.7 m, total about 3.6–7.9 m. No main circulation route should cut through it.
- **Working order:** fridge → prep surface → sink (dishwasher beside it) → prep → hob → serving. Leave at least 30–40 cm of worktop on each side of the hob. Don't put the hob under a window or at the end of a run.
- **Layouts:**
  - Single run (I): room width ≥ about 1.80 m (60 + 120).
  - Galley (parallel): width ≥ about 2.40 m.
  - L-shape.
  - U-shape: width ≥ about 2.40 m.
  - Island: room width ≥ about 3.60 m.
- **Turkish use.** A daily breakfast or meal table is expected (`01` §6). Plan its space, or a clear relationship to the dining area in open kitchens. Allow for a tall pantry unit or a kiler next door.

## 4. Bathrooms and WC

| Fixture | Size (cm) | Clearance in front | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| WC (floor or wall-hung) | about 36–40 × 53–70 | 60–70 | Centre ≥ 40 from a side wall. In Turkey a **taharet** bidet spray built into the WC is expected; plan the supply. |
| Basin | 50–60 wide, top at about 85 | 70 | Mirror and light above; socket for a hairdryer away from water |
| Shower | 80 × 80 minimum, 90 × 90 or 90 × 120 comfortable; walk-in 100 × 140+ | 70 | Floor falls 1–2% to a drain (yer süzgeci). Linear drains allow a level threshold. |
| Bathtub | 70 × 160/170 | 70 along the long side | Less common in new flats; showers dominate |
| Washing machine (if in the bathroom) | 60 × 60 | 70 | Needs supply, drain and a socket out of the wet zone. Turkish flats often put it here or on the balcony. |

- **Typical sizes (rule of thumb):**
  - main bathroom in a 2+1: about 4–6 m²
  - en-suite (ebeveyn banyosu): about 3–4 m²
  - guest WC: about 1.2–2.0 m²
- **Placement:**
  - Bathroom doors should not open into the kitchen or salon.
  - A guest WC should be reachable from the antre.
  - Wet rooms cluster on shafts and stack vertically (`06`).
- **Ventilation.** A window, or mechanical extract. The regulation minimum window is in `09`.

## 5. Bedrooms

- **Master bedroom:** 12–16 m². A 160 bed against a solid wall, 60–70 clear on both sides, and a wardrobe 2.4–3.0 m long. The en-suite door should not open toward the bed.
- **Child's or second bedroom:** 9–12 m². A 90 bed, a desk 120 × 60 with chair space, and a wardrobe 1.2–1.8 m long.
- **Test every bedroom with furniture drawn.** One wall must take the bed head without a door or window. Doors and the wardrobe must not fight.
- **Windows.** A sill of about 90 cm suits beds and desks. Put a blind or shutter box in the wall build-up.

## 6. Living spaces (salon)

- **Width.** A sofa facing a TV with a coffee table between needs about 3.60–4.20 m clear. Under about 3.40 m the room feels like a corridor.
- **Circulation.** Paths should pass beside the seating group, not through it. Doors at one end keep the opposite end usable.
- **Dining.** Put it between the kitchen and the salon, or as part of an open kitchen.
- **Balcony.** Connect the balcony to the salon. Useful depth is 1.50 m or more (1.20 m minimum for two chairs).

## 7. Antre, storage, utility

- **Antre:** about 1.5–2.0 m wide at the door. A shoe cabinet 35–40 deep, coat storage 60 deep, a place to sit or a mirror. The sightline from the door should not land on a bathroom or bedroom.
- **Kiler or storage:** 1.5–3 m² in 2+1 and larger flats. Also a cleaning cupboard for vacuum, ironing board and mop.
- **Laundry:** a machine (and dryer) in the bathroom, a utility niche, or the balcony with a supply and drain. Plan a place to dry laundry that isn't the street façade.

## 8. Doors and windows (Turkish practice)

- **Door openings (kasa) are written width/height in cm:**
  - interior 80/210 or 90/210
  - flat entrance (steel door) 100/210
  - balcony door 80–90/210–220
  - legal minimums in `09`
- **Door swing.** Interior doors open into the room, against a wall. The handle side gets about 30–45 cm beside it for reach (more for accessibility). Doors should not collide with each other or with fixtures.
- **Window sills (rule of thumb):**
  - living room 40–90
  - bedroom about 90
  - kitchen about 105–110, above the worktop
  - bathroom 150–180, for privacy
  - full-height glazing to a balcony from 0

  Low sills and full-height glazing need guarding. Read the current PAİY text for the guard height. Window heads are usually about 2.10–2.40 m.
- **Opening type.** Turkish PVC and aluminium windows are usually tilt-and-turn (vasistas + açılır). Leave space for an inward-opening sash: no radiator, shelf or cabinet in its swing.

## 9. Stairs inside a flat or villa

- **Private stairs:** risers up to about 18–19 cm, treads 26–28 cm, comfort check 2R + G ≈ 60–64 cm, width 0.90–1.00 m or more, headroom ≥ about 2.10 m measured vertically from the stair nosing line.
- **Common stairs** follow the regulation (`09`) and the fire rules (`06`).
- **Size the stair from arithmetic, not a guess.** Risers = floor-to-floor ÷ riser height, rounded up. Each flight's length = (risers in the flight − 1) × tread. Add landings at least as deep as the stair width. (Worked example in `design-reasoning`.)

## 10. Accessibility in housing

- **Required:**
  - a step-free building entrance or ramp
  - lift access where a lift is required
  - door widths and landings per TS 9111
  - accessible parking per the Otopark Yönetmeliği

  Figures are in `09`.
- **Good practice even where not required:** at least one bathroom per flat with a Ø 150 cm turning circle or the potential for one, level thresholds, sockets and switches at 40–120 cm. It costs little at design stage and a lot later.
