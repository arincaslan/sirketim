# Interior architecture

The department's scope includes interiors (`departments/architecture/CLAUDE.md`). In Turkey interior architects have their own chamber, the **İç Mimarlar Odası**, which publishes *İçmimari Proje Çizim ve Sunuş Standartları*. Dimensions for furniture, kitchens and bathrooms are in `03-space-planning-ergonomics.md`; materials and build-ups in `04-construction-tr.md`.

## 1. Process

1. **Brief.** Who lives or works there, daily routines, storage needs, budget, style references, what stays and what goes.
2. **Measured survey (rölöve).** Measure every wall, opening, ceiling height, beam, column, riser, socket, radiator, floor level and window sill. Take photos of every wall. Never design an interior from a sales brochure plan; they are rarely accurate to the centimetre.
3. **Concept.** A mood board, a material board (3–5 main materials) and a colour palette. Two options, with the reasoning stated.
4. **Space plan.** Furniture layout with circulation tested (clearances in `03`). Demolition and new-wall plan (yıkılacak / yapılacak duvarlar) where walls change. Check structure before removing any wall: shear walls (perde) and columns can never be removed.
5. **Design development.** For each room:
   - floor plan
   - reflected ceiling plan with levels
   - lighting plan
   - socket and switch plan
   - wall elevations (iç görünüş / açılım)
   - tiling layouts for wet rooms
6. **Technical package.**
   - millwork details at 1/10–1/1
   - door and finish schedules (mahal listesi)
   - FF&E list: furniture, fixtures and equipment, with supplier, code, size, finish, quantity and price
   - specifications
7. **Budget and procurement.** Get quotes, order long-lead items early (custom joinery, imported tiles, stone).
8. **Site.** Check setting-out before tiling and joinery. Then the snag list and handover.

## 2. Lighting design

- **Layers:**
  - ambient (general)
  - task (worktop, mirror, desk, reading)
  - accent (art, niches, texture)
  - decorative (pendants)

  Put each layer on its own circuit, and use dimming in living and bedrooms.
- **Typical illuminance targets (lux):**
  - living room 100–300 (dimmable)
  - dining table 200–300
  - kitchen worktop 300–500
  - bathroom mirror 300–500, measured on the face
  - bedroom general 100–200, reading 300–500
  - home office desk 500 (office standard EN 12464-1)
  - corridors and stairs 100–150
- **Colour temperature:** 2700–3000 K for living and sleeping; 3000–4000 K for kitchens, bathrooms and work areas.
- **Colour rendering:** CRI ≥ 80, and ≥ 90 where colour matters (kitchen, bathroom, wardrobe, retail).
- **Glare and placement.**
  - Keep downlights away from directly above seated heads and from reflecting in the TV.
  - Light the mirror from the sides or front, not only from above.
  - Use linear light under wall cabinets.
- **Wet zones.** Fittings in and near showers need ingress protection appropriate to the zone (about IP44 near, IP65 in the spray zone; IEC 60364-7-701 zones). The electrical engineer confirms.

## 3. Sockets, switches, data (rules of thumb)

| Location | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Salon | 4–6 double sockets including the TV wall (power + data + antenna), sockets by sofa ends, dimmer circuits |
| Bedroom | Double socket each side of the bed; desk sockets; two-way switching at door and bed |
| Kitchen | A socket about every 1–1.2 m of worktop (at about 110 cm). Dedicated circuits for oven, hob, dishwasher and fridge; hood socket above the hob. |
| Bathroom | Shaver/hair-dryer socket away from water; washing-machine circuit; heated towel rail |
| Heights | Sockets about 30–40 cm; switches about 105–120 cm; worktop sockets about 110 cm |

## 4. Materials and performance

| Need | Check |
|---|---|
| Floor wear | Ceramic and porcelain: PEI or abrasion class for the room use. Laminate: AC3 for homes, AC4–AC5 for heavy or commercial use. |
| Slip resistance | Wet areas and outdoor surfaces: R10–R11 (DIN 51130 classes) or the equivalent |
| Water and humidity | Moisture-resistant boards (green drywall) and waterproofed substrates in wet rooms (`04` §6). No MDF cabinet carcasses near showers. |
| Fire | Escape routes and public interiors need finishes of the fire class the Yangın Yönetmeliği requires. Check before specifying wood or fabric wall linings in common areas or shops. |
| Acoustics | Hard minimalist interiors echo. Add rugs, curtains, upholstered pieces, acoustic panels, and underlay under timber floors (`05` §7). |
| Maintenance | Matte dark surfaces show dust and fingerprints. High-gloss lacquer scratches. Light grout stains. Choose for the user's real life. |

**Joinery (mobilya / dolap):**
- Carcass in 18 mm melamine chipboard (yonga levha) or MDF.
- Fronts in lacquer (lake), acrylic, PVC membrane, laminate or veneer.
- Concealed hinges and full-extension drawers.
- Worktops in quartz (kuvars), compact laminate, granite or porcelain slab.
- Concealed WC cisterns (gömme rezervuar) need a 15–20 cm build-out wall. Plan it in the space plan, not on site.

## 5. Colour and composition

- **The 60-30-10 rule:** 60% dominant (walls, floors), 30% secondary (furniture, textiles), 10% accent.
- Keep undertones consistent: warm with warm, cool with cool, with one deliberate contrast.
- **Orientation changes colour.** North light is cool, so warmer tones work. South and west light is warm and strong, so cooler or muted tones work. Judge samples in the actual room's light, not on screen.
- Limit the palette to 3–5 materials per space, and repeat them between rooms for continuity.

## 6. Show flats and sales catalogues

- Stage for the target buyer's life: a dining table in the kitchen, desks in children's rooms, a set balcony. Visibly real storage sells better than empty minimalism.
- The render pipeline is Blender for geometry, then OpenArt to polish (department CLAUDE.md). The interior render must match the drawn plan and dimensions. A render that shows a room larger than the plan is misrepresentation.
- **Every render is marketing collateral.** Label it as illustrative, and match the furniture to the dimensions actually available.

## 7. Interior drawing set (minimum)

1. Survey plan (rölöve), with the date and who measured it
2. Demolition and construction plan, if walls change, with structural walls marked untouchable
3. Furniture layout plan
4. Flooring plan (materials, start lines, thresholds, level changes)
5. Reflected ceiling plan (levels, suspended ceilings, coves, access hatches)
6. Lighting plan (fittings, circuits, switching)
7. Electrical and data plan (sockets, heights, dedicated circuits)
8. Wall elevations for every room with joinery, tiles or special finishes
9. Wet-room tiling layouts
10. Joinery and special details (1/10–1/1)
11. Door schedule, finish schedule (mahal listesi), FF&E list
12. Material board
