# Architecture expertise

This skill is the department's professional knowledge. It covers the thinking that comes before and around the drawings: what a good building is, how buildings are built in Turkey, and what the regulations and the market expect. Three companion skills cover:
- the rules (`zoning-compliance-tr`)
- decisions (`design-reasoning`)
- production (`autocad-drafting`)

## How an experienced architect works (and how we should)

1. **Understand before drawing.** Settle the brief, the constraint ledger and the site analysis first (`01` §2–3). Most bad buildings are well-drawn answers to the wrong question.
2. **Think in section and three dimensions,** not only in plan. Stairs, ramps, heights, sun and neighbours live in section.
3. **Generate options, then decide with stated criteria** (`design-reasoning`). Write down why the others lost.
4. **Integrate early.** Structure grid, shafts, plant rooms, fire stairs and the envelope shape the plan. Added late, they break it (`06`).
5. **Draw the truth:**
   - real wall thicknesses (insulated external walls are about 30–35 cm)
   - real furniture
   - real door swings
   - areas computed from the drawing, never retyped
6. **Respect the user's culture and market** (`01` §6): antre and shoe storage, salon and guest separation, a kitchen with a table, balconies, storage.
7. **Run the critique checklist** (`01` §7) before anything is shown. "Fine" without a reason is not an answer.
8. **Be honest about status.**
   - Regulation figures are verified against the current text before they are cited.
   - Rules of thumb are labelled.
   - Everything we produce is schematic and needs licensed-architect review before permitting or construction (department rule).

## Confidence labels used in the reference files

- **rule of thumb:** professional practice for sizing and checking. Not a limit, not a citation.
- **search-verified (date):** a regulation figure confirmed in search results quoting the text or a professional summary. Fine for planning. **Read the official text before citing it in a deliverable.**
- **repo-verified:** read from the official text in earlier project work and recorded in this repo.
- **verify:** a known value not confirmed this session. A pointer only.

## Keeping the expertise growing

- **When a regulation changes,** update `09` only. The other files point to it, so there is one copy to keep current.
- **When a project teaches something,** add it to the relevant reference file with the date and source, so the next project starts from it. Examples: a plan-note trap, a detail that failed, a market fact, a municipal requirement.
- **What this base does not cover** (research these per project, and say so):
  - specialized building types (hospitals, schools, industry)
  - structural or MEP calculations (that is engineers' work)
  - restoration and listed buildings (koruma kurulu rules)
  - landscape design in depth
  - current market construction prices
