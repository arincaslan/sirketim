# Client Proposal

Produces a proposal under `departments/sales/proposals/<slug>.md`, per `departments/sales/CLAUDE.md`.

## Steps

1. **Intake**. Confirm which service(s) are in scope, the client's stated goals/budget/timeline, and anything already discussed. Don't invent scope or pricing the client hasn't agreed to explore.
2. **Cross-check scope**. For each department involved, read its CLAUDE.md so the proposal's scope and stack claims match what that department actually delivers (e.g. don't promise a stack web-development doesn't use, or a CAD deliverable architecture doesn't produce without licensed review).
3. **Draft** (`proposals/<slug>.md`): scope of work per department, deliverables, rough timeline, pricing structure (ranges are fine pre-negotiation), next steps.
4. **Register/update** the client in `shared/clients.md` and note the pipeline stage in `pipeline/<slug>.md` (create if new).
5. **Report back**: what's in the draft and what needs the founder's input before it goes out (final pricing, tone check, anything client-sensitive).

## Notes

- Never send/share the proposal externally — this skill only drafts it locally for the founder to review and send.
